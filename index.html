About Focus

Network for Ethics in Governance Research Initiative

Technology moves at lightning speed, but governance is stuck in the past. Traditional checklists cannot see the hidden risks of artificial intelligence.

At the Network for Ethics in Governance Research Initiative (NERI), we return to first principles. Guided by the timeless Tamil truth of Aram (righteousness) and Neri (the path), we tackle four defining questions for the age of AI:

  1. Unblemished Innovation: How do we ensure automated systems avoid hidden bias and systemic harm?
  2. Fiduciary Duty: When machines decide, how do board directors exercise real oversight instead of waiting for a crisis?
  3. Institutional Accountability: When systems fail, who answers—the software or the leaders?
  4. Enduring Legacy: How is leadership measured by lasting integrity rather than quarterly speed?

As Great Thirvalluvar taught:

அறன்ஈனும் இன்பமும் ஈனும் திறனறிந்து
தீதின்றி வந்த பொருள். (Kural 754)
Meaning: Wealth and technology gathered through blemish-less means bring lasting well-being.
தக்கார் தகவிலர் என்பது அவரவர்
எச்சத்தால் காணப் படும். (Kural 114)
Meaning: A leader's true character is judged forever by the legacy they leave behind.

NERI brings this ancient wisdom back into modern boardrooms and public institutions as the true pathway to righteousness.
